Learning-based control · Aerospace autonomy

Donghe Chen 陈东和

Ph.D. Student in Control Science and Engineering
School of Astronautics, Beihang University

I develop learning-based control methods for aerospace and robotic systems, with a focus on reinforcement learning, robustness and safety under dynamics uncertainty, real-time optimal control, and UAV autonomy.

Email Google Scholar IEEE ORCID GitHub

Research

Reliable learning for physical systems

Bridging data-driven decision making with control-theoretic structure for fast, robust, and deployable autonomy.

01

Reinforcement Learning

Learning efficient control policies for nonlinear, high-dimensional, and precision-critical systems.

02

Robust & Safe Control

Improving reliability under uncertain dynamics, disturbances, and rare failure-inducing conditions.

03

Real-Time Optimal Control

Combining analytical structure and learning to deliver high-quality decisions within onboard compute limits.

04

UAV & Aerospace Autonomy

Developing deployable methods for quadrotors, spacecraft, guidance, and reusable launch vehicles.
